MEMO · RE: Privacy

Privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Midlife Survivors is a free browser game made by Rami Daud. This page says, in plain words, what the game keeps and why. Short version: as little as possible, and nothing is sold or shared.

Without an account

  • Your progress (coins, unlocks, settings, a run in progress) is saved in your own browser on your own device. It never leaves it.
  • Anonymous play stats help me balance the game: how long a run lasts, how far you got, what got you, the game version, and whether you're on a phone or a laptop. They're tied to a random ID made by your browser, not to you.
  • The online leaderboard stores the name you type, your score, age reached and character. Only post a score if you're happy for that name to be public.
  • Visit counts come from GoatCounter, which doesn't use tracking cookies or collect personal information.

If you clock in (optional)

  • Signing in is never required. It only lets your progress follow you to other devices.
  • Email sign-in: we keep your email address so we can send you a sign-in link or code. No newsletters or marketing.
  • Google or Apple sign-in: we receive your email address and the basic profile details those services share (such as your name). We only use your email to identify your account.
  • Your cloud save holds the same progress your browser already keeps, plus the game version and whether you were on a phone or a laptop.
  • Accounts and cloud saves are stored with Supabase, the service that runs the game's database. Each player can only read and change their own save.

Deleting your data

  • In the game, open Clock in on your employee file and choose Delete my cloud save. That removes it right away.
  • To delete your account entirely, or anything else (like a leaderboard entry), message me on the Midlife Survivors Discord and I'll take care of it.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device.

Kids

The game is about surviving midlife and isn't aimed at children under 13. Please don't create an account if you're under 13.

Changes

If this changes, the date at the top changes too, and the game's "What's new" notes will say so.

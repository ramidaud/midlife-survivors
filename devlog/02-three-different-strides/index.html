The office has people in it now. And they walk.

Oct 31, 2026 V. 1.23.4
▶ Play this build

What changed

  • Four-direction walking sheets for all three playable characters
  • Explore As switcher and direction gallery in the art test
  • Step timing tied to distance traveled
Next upJudge the costumes and rhythm, then idle and combat poses.

The office layout felt good, but I wanted the graphics to feel more animated. So the next art pass was walking sheets for all three playable characters: the 9-to-5er, the Soccer Dad and the Grad Student at 45.

They each have four directions and four walking poses per direction. You can switch between them in the separate art test and walk around the same desks. There's also a side-by-side preview so I can compare their movement before replacing the main game's art.

One useful detail: the walking animation follows distance traveled. If you push into a desk and stop moving, your feet stop too. Small thing, but it helps the character feel connected to the room.

These are the first walking sheets. The costumes and rhythm still need a look, then we can add idle and combat reactions.

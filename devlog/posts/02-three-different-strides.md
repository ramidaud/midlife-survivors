The office layout felt good, but I wanted the graphics to feel more animated. So the next art pass was walking sheets^[A sprite sheet is one image holding every frame of an animation in a grid. The game shows one square at a time, fast, like a flip book.] for all three playable characters: the 9-to-5er, the Soccer Dad and the Grad Student at 45.

They each have four directions and four walking poses per direction. You can switch between them in the separate art test and walk around the same desks. There's also a side-by-side preview so I can compare their movement before replacing the main game's art.

One useful detail: the walking animation follows distance traveled.^[Most games loop the walk on a timer, so a character pushing into a wall moonwalks in place. Counting steps by distance fixes that.] If you push into a desk and stop moving, your feet stop too. Small thing, but it helps the character feel connected to the room.

These are the first walking sheets. The costumes and rhythm still need a look, then we can add idle and combat reactions.

## It was just a lot of fun to play in a group

The original Deep Rock Galactic is what my little gaming group plays. Other than the fact that it's always a good social interaction, the game just stood out as different visually and mechanics-wise. And it was just a lot of fun to play in a group.

Seeing it go into the Survivor mode brought a lot of those elements into a single-player game.^[Survivor is a top-down spin-off of the first-person original, built on the Vampire Survivors formula. Funday started it after a conversation about Vampire Survivors with Ghost Ship's CEO at Gamescom 2022. More on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_Rock_Galactic:_Survivor).] I wouldn't actually mind seeing the Survivor take on a multiplayer aspect. I think this would be a really fun experience to share with someone else, or a group of people. I certainly would consider that for future versions of Midlife.

## A very clear vision

They have a very clear vision for how their upgrades and perks and weapons work. I think it might be useful for me to really explore that.

![A level-up screen in Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor: three upgrade cards marked Common, Legendary and Uncommon, each with its bonus.](img/18-drg-level-up.jpg "side")

Every card tells you its rarity by color, what it does in one line, and which weapon it's for. Between stages there's a shop with the same cards and a price on each.

![The shop between stages: five upgrade cards with rarities, prices, a reroll and a heal.](img/18-drg-shop.jpg)

I also just absolutely love the graphics, and the ability to get away from the enemies by mining through rock and creating little tunnels and funnels, to push the enemies through to help you get away or to attack them more easily.

The difficulty felt very appropriate. It didn't absolutely destroy me immediately, but it definitely was a challenge.

## I want to take it to that next level

I've hoped to make a 3D version of Midlife for a while. Definitely a kind of three-quarter top-down level with multiple heights and places to explore. Really great animation art for the enemies, as well as our heroes and the bosses.

I want to take it to that next level, but I don't want to undersell the pixel art thing I've been building this whole time. I'm really enjoying that as well.

## Maybe you can cut through the forests

One thing I want to try in Midlife now: maybe in Maple Hollow you can cut through the forests. Right now the trees around the town are a solid wall. Deep Rock lets you carve your own way out of a bad spot, and Maple Hollow could use something that only happens there.

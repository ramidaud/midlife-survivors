The office got bigger. Somebody lost a binder.

Nov 01, 2026 V. 1.27.1

Pixel-art cover illustration: the 9-to-5er takes the long way to coffee through the office, with Bills, a spam phone and a donut trailing him.

TThe office was starting to look like an office, but it still felt like a little room. I wanted somewhere I could wander.

The art preview now has an atrium, longer paths, and side wings. The map fills in as you walk. There is a records annex with a lost binder, which feels like the right thing to find in a place like this.

The floor got some attention too: brighter carpet, worn chair mats, coffee rings, and a printer paper trail. Enough history to feel used, with room left to see whatever is chasing me.

This is still the separate art preview. Next we need to see what happens when a crowd tries to get around those desks.

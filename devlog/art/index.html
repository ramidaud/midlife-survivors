Spotlight cards

When a boss walks in, a coworker opens a new room or a run starts, a big card hops into the game. This is the real thing, running on this page.

The animation: a light beam opens, the band wipes in, the character hops up and lands in a puff of dust, their name drops in letter by letter and their line types itself out. Bosses get a red beam and cut in line; everyone else waits their turn.

Fighting room with things to notice

A roomy start

Lobby and Bullpen are open at 40. About 7.6 desktop camera areas of connected floor before furniture. Three differently shaped fighting reserves stay free of solid props.

Dressed open space

13 reserves: queue tape, rugs, chair-mat wear, coffee rings, paper trails and floor decals. Some split in two or sit off-center.

Hallways have stories

Vending alcoves, lost-and-found, a sad birthday banner and an unplugged lamp. Solids belong in side bays, clear of the 224px main route.

A modular kit, then native-size review

72 planned prop families with two or three color variants, nine seamless 64×64 floor families and six wall/cubicle/glass segments. The first 13 prop families are generated; the remaining families and floor kit are planned. Stage 2 will assemble instances from prop IDs, positions, variants and floor footprints.

Every new prop must appear beside these real sprites, at 1× on desktop and a 390px phone. Native prop comparisons are in the Stage 2A review.

Runtime target: no more than about 120 visible props, off-screen culling, smooth play with 350 enemies. Geometry is checked here; that performance test belongs to implementation.

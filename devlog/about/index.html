PERSONNEL FILE · RE: Rami Daud

About Rami

Rami Daud is a photographer who decided, somewhere in his forties, to make a video game.

He has spent more than 20 years behind the camera: freelancing for the Associated Press, AFP and Cleveland Magazine, four years as a digital imaging specialist at NASA Glenn Research Center on the Artemis and Commercial Crew programs, and since 2019 as Senior Photographer at Kent State University.

He is also a grad student in Kent State's Emerging Media and Technology program, and he writes Framing the Cosmos, about how iconic space photographs shape the way we see the universe.

Midlife Survivors is his first game. He brings the vision and the jokes; Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok help him build it. This devlog is the record of him learning how.

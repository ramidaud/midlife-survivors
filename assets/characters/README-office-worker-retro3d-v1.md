# 9-to-5er retro rendered skin v1

Cosmetic review draft, art only. `office-worker-retro3d-walk-56x60-v1.png` is exactly 448x300: 8 columns, 5 rows, 56x60 cells. Rows 0-3 walk DOWN, UP, LEFT, RIGHT; row 4 uses cells 0-3 to breathe/blink and cells 4-7 to push his glasses up. Mug retained. Sole line y56, three clear pixels below. No floor shadow.

The finish deliberately differs from the normal cast: faceted, low-poly shoulders/head, stepped rendered shading, plastic highlights on hair and shoes, darker material edges instead of the usual continuous navy outline. Neutral white balance. Game palette plus six intermediate shades: #26304A, #697789, #B5956C, #D87849, #F7E4BB, #6A463A. Exact opaque #FF00FF background, no anti-aliased halo.

Five row GIFs at approximately 75 ms per frame, enlarged 3x; `-check.png` is the full sheet at 2x. Native dimensions, palette, opacity and populated cells validated. Existing hero sheets and runtime files untouched. Not wired into the cosmetics shop.

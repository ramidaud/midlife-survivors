# Maple Hollow house fronts v1

Local art-only draft. `maple-hollow-houses-v1.png`: 1152x224, six 192x224 cells. Porch/front ground edge y216, centered at x96. Four-pixel minimum side clearance. Full magenta atlas has no lawn, street or cast shadow. `-yard-preview.png` places all six in one row over the native Maple Hollow grass, sidewalk and curb tiles. `-check.png` is a nearest-neighbor 4x sheet check.

Cell order: cream HOA colonial; sage ranch/clutter garage/leaf-filled gutter/netless hoop; pumpkin-heavy russet split-level/inflatable cat; navy craftsman/porch swing/chime/faceless sitter; lavender cottage/three cats/feeders/fence/birdbath; slate renovation/tarp/dumpster/ladder.

The first broad study made doors too small. Final art uses the taller, narrower correction, sampled proportionally into the native cells without stretching. One shared scale preserves single-story versus two-story height differences. Twelve-color palette and opaque exact #FF00FF background. No text or house numbers. Faceless window silhouettes are fictional details. Placement, footprints and collision remain for Claude.

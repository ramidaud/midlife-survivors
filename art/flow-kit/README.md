## Scene prompts
Build every scene prompt in the same order: **who, where, what happens, camera, style line, length.** Name characters exactly as saved.

**Title loop (test this first):**
```
The 9-to-5er walks into a cozy pixel-art office lobby at dawn, holding his coffee mug, and stops to take a sip.
Behind him, a few Bills peek out from behind a cubicle wall.
Camera: slow side-scrolling pan, fixed height, like a 2D game.
16-bit pixel art, chunky navy outlines, warm muted palette, soft morning light through windows.
8 seconds, seamless loop, no text, no camera shake.
```

## Tips
- **Keep the camera flat and sideways.** Pixel art survives slow pans. Fast spins and zooms make the AI redraw the characters and they drift.
- **Ask for "no text."** Video models scribble fake words onto signs.
- **2 or 3 characters per shot, max.** More than that and the faces start blending.
- **Save the good takes, toss the rest.** Send me the keepers; I'll shrink them, snap them back to a pixel grid and loop them for the game.

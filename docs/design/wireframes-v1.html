Title screen

One primary. The art gets the room. The same order of parts on every device.

RegionWhat goes thereThumb or controller
1Key artRotating title art. Decoration only, never a tap target.—

3PUNCH INThe one primary. Starts the run.Thumb zone on phones. A or Enter.
4Today's shiftA Row showing stage and mode. Opens a small sheet: location, mode, sprint, tax.Tap. Down, then A.
5Employee cardsFour tile Cards. Selected has the coffee border.Tap. Left and right.
6Bottom bar / top navShop, Records, Devlog, Settings. Bottom bar on phones, top nav elsewhere.Tap. Bumpers (LB/RB).
7Devlog memoMemo Card on paper. On phones it lives in the Devlog tab.Tap opens the memo.
8Toast slotWhat's New, welcome back. One at a time, leaves on its own.Tap to open, or ignore.

Settings (today's Options, plus the settings inside Pause)

One place for sound, game and controls. Reached from the title and from Pause.

RegionWhat goes thereThumb or controller
1HeaderMemo strip and title.—
2SectionsSound, Game, Controls. Tabs on phones and tablets, a list on wide screens.Tap. Bumpers switch section.
3RowsOne setting per Row with a switch, stepper or value. Plain names ("Confirm: A").Tap. Left and right change a value.
4ControlsShows the input you are using now: touch, keys or controller.A on a Row rebinds it.
5Action barDONE (primary). RESET TO DEFAULTS (danger, asks twice).B or Esc saves and closes.
6LegendController and key hints. Hidden on touch.—

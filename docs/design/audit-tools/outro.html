Your open decisions

Grok's seven plus Gemini's one, with what the screenshots say

DecisionSuggestedWhat the audit adds
1. One headline and one ticker at a time. One total on a phone during a boss.YesThe boss rows show five text styles in one frame. This is the single biggest fix.
2. Boss intro card: 3.5 seconds, or delay the first attack.ShortenAgree, and on phones use a compact band with no floor dim. The dim is what hides the first attack.
3. Rewards never take the center.YesThe center flash has no backing plate and is hard to read on the tan floor. Fewer things should use it.
4. Hold errands during bosses and off milestone ages.YesOn a phone the errand strip sits about 50 px above the hero for up to 45 seconds.
5. Pause swarm events during the Review, Mortgage and V8.YesThe age 45 row is that collision.
6. First modifier roll at 50.8.YesNo screenshot needed. It only moves a pile-up apart.
7. Phone center banner at 18% height.My call: not 18%Move it, but to a slot directly under the HUD, with a backing plate. At 18% it lands on the minimap (which starts at x 212 on a phone). In the spec the minimap gives way while a headline is up.
8. Plain verbs on buttons (PAUSE, REROLL, SKIP), joke in the subtitle.Plain verbsToday's labels already run long: BACK TO MENU (SPACE / ENTER / ESC / A). The humor is strong in the lines under the buttons. Keep it there.

What I could not capture

  • The Workload chip. It only draws in a real Overtime run. Its style is read from the code.
  • Phone landscape. Not in the brief's four sizes. The spec will cover it.
  • Real notches and home bars. Headless browsers have no safe areas. That needs your phone.
  • Controller focus on every menu. I checked the pause menu only.
  • The live leaderboard and share pages (network blocked on purpose).

Two homes

Top, under the HUD: alarms. At rest this zone holds the status row and the minimap. When a headline arrives on a phone they step aside until it leaves.

Bottom: story. The ticker. The spotlight band uses the same home and replaces the ticker while it is up.

The middle belongs to the game. No text, dim or card covers the floor around the hero during a boss.

On touch phones the ticker floats 96 px above the bottom, so your thumb rests under it and not on it.

The queue

{{T32}}

No cutting in

A message never replaces one of equal or higher tier. It waits, or it is dropped when stale. A late warning is wrong information.

Quiet window

Nothing new starts while a boss is winding up or attacking, plus 300 ms. Low health is the one exception.

Targets

4 messages a minute on average. 6 in any minute at most. 40% of every minute with only the HUD on screen.

One look (design system v1, Nov 2026)

The game and the devlog are one product. The full spec is docs/design/design-system-v1.md.
  • Tokens only. Colors, type sizes, spacing, corners, layers and timings come from assets/ui/ms-tokens.css. No new hex values, pixel sizes or z-index numbers in code.
  • Fonts: Silkscreen for labels of four words or fewer, in caps. Atkinson Hyperlegible for anything you read. Nothing you read is under 14 px. Nothing you tap is under 44 px.
  • Colors: the devlog palette only. Coffee orange fill means the one primary button on a screen. Russet and teal never carry small text.
  • Eight parts: Button, Row, Card, Sheet, Banner, Toast, Chip, Memo. If a new surface can't be built from these, raise it with Rami before inventing a ninth.
  • In-run messages go through the router. Alarms in the headline slot under the HUD, story in the ticker at the bottom. One of each at most, one total on a phone during a boss. Only sheets and moments pause. Nothing covers the floor around the hero during a boss.
  • Three size classes: compact, regular, wide, plus a short flag for phones held sideways. No other breakpoints.
  • Voice: plain verbs on buttons, the joke in the line underneath. No emoji in gameplay text.
  • Every screen is checked at phone, iPad (both orientations) and desktop sizes, with touch, keyboard and a controller, before it ships.
  • The devlog memo, social image and social kit bullets stay as they are.

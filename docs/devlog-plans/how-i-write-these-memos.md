# Future memo: how I write these memos (planned Nov 6)

Rami's idea after Memo 12. Show readers exactly how the memos get made: his real words, edited back and forth with Claude, plus the design choices made along the way. Openly AI-assisted, like every memo.

## The angle
- The words are mine. Claude organizes, checks facts, fills in build details and suggests options. I pick, cut and rewrite.
- A memo is iterated like a game build: draft, playtest (read it), feedback, change, repeat.
- Memo 12 is the case study: 22 saved revisions over about 9 hours (midnight to 9 AM, with sleep in between), growing from about 780 to about 1,900 words.

## What actually happened on Memo 12 (the evidence)
- **Raw material:** six interview questions answered in rough one-liners at midnight, plus a Discord thread, a Facebook comment, a desk photo and three book links.
- **Title:** "Most of this didn't start with me." → "Somewhere to put it." → "Restless, and who's actually playing." → "Restless. Also, who's actually playing?" (Rami: the first one "feels very AI".)
- **Intro:** Claude's first version, then four options, then Rami combined three of them into his own. Then Claude merged the overlap.
- **Rami's line, kept on purpose:** "I'm not great at sitting still, and this gives me somewhere to put it."
- **Word fixes from Rami:** "holistic instead of chaotic" → "holistic and chaotic" → a clearer sentence when it was flagged as confusing. "Last night" → "Tonight". Hanna introduced as an industry veteran first, friend's wife second.
- **Honesty fixes:** "real music" → "AI-generated" with the actual prompts shown.
- **Structure:** Rami reordered the sections (who's playing first, then the people, then the music) and moved the reading list.
- **Design choices made mid-edit:** prompt cards styled like code, audio players, book covers, drop caps (Silkscreen, then a serif pixel font with a light shadow), What Changed moved to the side column, social buttons, a Ben mugshot in a sidenote.
- **Things left out on purpose:** private DMs, the commenter's name, a too-honest answer Rami chose to soften himself.

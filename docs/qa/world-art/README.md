# Hero and office integration QA, V. 1.28.13 (Codex)

The approved art preview is now used by the production game. Tests run in the local HTTP game with in-memory saves and all leaderboard requests intercepted by the diagnostic harness. No test scores were submitted. Direct-file browser testing remains unavailable through this browser tool.

- Hero: four direction samples returned frames 7 (up), 13 (right), 1 (down), 9 (left). All three character keys were inspected, each using a 56x60 visual frame. Original player body stays 48x52. Stopping holds a standing frame; the original sprite continues to own movement, health and collisions.
- Office: nine pooled 3072x2048 seven-area sections, 360 scenery bodies, no old mezzanine objects. Furniture uses the approved floor footprints. The main base floor sits behind the imported carpet, wood, wear, lighting and contact layers.
- Collision: approaching a desk from below stopped the player at y=-324, retaining the 48x52 body. Placing the hero behind its upper illustration faded the foreground paperwork desk to alpha 0.48. It did not disable the desk body.
- Seam: shifting one campus width to x2304 retained 360 props, nine nearby props and aligned body centers. Main routes continue across section boundaries; the isolated Art Room keeps its original bounded presentation. Camera-relative actor depths avoid unbounded world coordinates overtaking HUD layers.
- Performance: six-second 350-enemy animation/AI stress fixture averaged 99.77 FPS, minimum 99.18 on this computer/browser. All four Bill frames were observed; 315 looping animated sprites were active at capture, including pings. Physics stepping was paused in the stress fixture to hold the crowd in view. This is a rendering/AI benchmark, not a full collision benchmark or a guarantee for physical phones. Off-camera sections are skipped only by the renderer; world visibility/physics state remains intact.
- Normal play: 88.04 seconds at Tax Bracket 0 reached age 41.956 with 12 HP and 84 enemies at capture, about 100 FPS, no captured errors and zero leaderboard requests. Normal movement and first-choice level-ups were automated; no combat values or ages were edited.
- Fallback: invalid printer image data left the original 25 props and nine platforms intact while the new hero remained available. Forcing both art flags off yielded the original hero (no walk sprite), 25 props and nine platforms. Expected bad-image warning is limited to the deliberate missing-office test.
- Phone: 390x844 viewport check, direction/character and office inspection, no horizontal overflow. Desktop comparison is 1280x800. Screenshots and JSON records are alongside this README.
- Lifecycle: scene restart is checked with the repeating office and hero active, retaining the expected prop count and original body. No duplicate hero draw is expected; shutdown removes the art update listener and late image imports ignore a stopped scene.

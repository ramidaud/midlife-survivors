Game dev roadmap · Age 40 to 50

Midlife Survivors

A Vampire Survivors-style game where you dodge emails, bills and back twinges with coffee, dad jokes and the word "No." Built in the browser with Phaser 3, one 45-90 minute step at a time, with a playable link at the end of every session.

How a session goes

  1. Claude recaps where you left off.
  2. You agree on one small step.
  3. Claude builds it; you play the link.
  4. You react like a player: too slow, too easy, funny?
  5. One new concept goes into your learning notes.

What Claude needs from you

  • Your jokes. The annoyances and moments that make midlife funny to you.
  • Your reactions. Play each build for five minutes and say how it feels.
  • Your eye. Art direction is yours to call in Phase 4.
  • Permission to delete files in the project folder when asked, so save points (git) can work.

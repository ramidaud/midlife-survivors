## Facebook (13-side-characters.jpg or the carousel)
New memo is up, and this one is about music.

Every morning I drive Abe to school and we listen to music together. Somewhere in those car rides I noticed his interest turning into something real. He taught himself to read music, plays guitar and keyboard, and picked up the clarinet for the middle school band. I'm proud of him.

Music comforts me when I'm sad, drives me when I'm focused, and gives me joy when I need it. I want the game to feel that way too. So the memo has the songs that are the game to me, plus an honest ask: I know what I like, I'm just not sure how to get there. If you know music and want to help, tell me.

## LinkedIn (13-side-characters.jpg)
I'm a photographer building my first video game, Midlife Survivors, with AI as my co-developer. Memo 13 of the devlog is about the part I understand least: music.

I know what I want the soundtrack to feel like. Slightly cacophonous but still joyful and energetic, sitting under all the sound effects without fighting them. I just don't know how to get there yet. That's been the honest lesson of this whole project: knowing your taste and having the craft are two different things, and it's OK to ask for help with the second one.

If you work in music or sound design and this sounds fun, I'd love to talk.

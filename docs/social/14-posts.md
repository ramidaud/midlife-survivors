## LinkedIn (14-achievement-unlocked-gaussian-splatting.jpg)
After more than 20 years as a photographer, I made my first 3D scene this week.

Gaussian splatting turns a set of ordinary photos into a 3D scene that renders in real time. I shot 212 frames of my son on a Nikon Z9, solved the camera positions, trained the splat on my laptop and directed camera moves in Blender.

What surprised me most: it all felt like photography. I chose the subject, took the photos, processed them and edited the final video. AI taught me the process and pointed me toward the right tools. I never lost ownership of the work.

I can see a place for this in science communication, maybe as a portrait series. For now, the full process and a shareable handoff file are in Memo 14 of my Midlife Survivors devlog.

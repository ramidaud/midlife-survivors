# Final boss: The Gold Watch (plan, Nov 3, 2026)

Rami picked **The Gold Watch** as Midlife mode's final boss: the classic retirement gift, except it's huge and ticking **down**. The death joke hides in plain sight: "Time's up."

## Where it goes
- **Midlife mode (40 to 65):** it arrives at about **63.5**, about 70 seconds before retirement. Survive or beat it and the retirement party at 65 plays.
- **Full Life mode** keeps "Father Time" at 100 for now; the watch can come back there later.

## Claude's starting design (Grok tunes the numbers, Gemini writes the words, ChatGPT draws it)
- **Body:** a giant gold pocket watch with a chain, floating slowly toward the middle of the room. It doesn't chase hard; the hands do the work.
- **Attacks:**
  1. **Minute Hand:** a long hand sweeps around the watch like a beam (warning: a faint line and tick marks first). Dodge by moving with it or crossing behind it.
  2. **Hour Hand:** shorter, thicker and slower. Joins in phase 2.
  3. **Alarm:** the bell rings out shockwave rings with a gap at the 12 o'clock position (like the Mortgage's % gap).
  4. **Loose Gears:** small gears and springs pop out and bounce.
- **Phases by health:** Ticking (100–60%: minute hand + gears) → Overtime (60–25%: both hands + alarm) → Last Minute (under 25%: everything faster, ticking speeds up).
- **Beat it:** the hands stop at 5:00, it chimes, drops a boss chest and becomes your keepsake on the retirement screen.
- **If it's still up at 65:** open question for Grok. Claude's proposal: you still retire (you clocked out), but only beating it earns the keepsake, an achievement and bonus 401(k).

## Update (Nov 3, V. 1.73.1): it can't be beaten
Rami: "Should we be able to kill the watch though? The most you can do in Vampire Survivors is keep him away a little longer." So now:
- No health. Your hits **wind it back**: a shove away from you (6 px/s per damage, capped) and its hands slow to 85% for 0.4 s.
- It arrives **1.5 years before retirement** (63.5 in a normal Midlife run, later with Delayed Retirement). Phases follow the clock: Overtime after 0.5 years, The Last Minute after 1.1.
- At 65 the hands stop at 5:00 on their own: tier-5 chest, +35 401(k), "Five O'Clock Somewhere", the keepsake, and "Keep The Receipt" if it never landed a hit.

# Pickup and projectile shape studies

The built-in imagegen tool used the field-item sheet as a style reference. The final assets were redrawn on a native pixel grid.

## Pickups I

Art direction study for the Midlife Survivors 32x32 native-pixel sprite atlas, with the attached field item sprites as exact style reference. Single horizontal row of isolated assets, generous gaps, fully transparent canvas. Navy one-pixel outline, hard clusters, no blur or shadow under the sprite, readable in miniature, warm office satire. Palette #171C35 #303750 #526078 #3E7775 #83A89A #D3B78D #F2D7A1 #FFF0CF #EF9347 #C45C4B #BFA8CF #F1B5BF. Three objects left to right: bright faceted teal XP crystal with cream glint; thick gold coin face stamped 401k in readable 3x5 pixel type; single triangular pepperoni pizza slice with a gooey cheese drip.
